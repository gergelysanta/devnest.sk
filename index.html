The apps

Small apps, one job each

Both of them take something you already have and show it to you properly: the working day you have just spent, and the files scattered across your disks.

Tracktiv
In beta

A time tracker for people who keep three projects going at once. It records the working day while the day happens and hands it back at the end of it — no timers, no account, nothing leaves the Mac.

Look at Tracktiv
AssetScout
On the Mac App Store

Point it at a folder or a whole drive and it lays out every picture, video, recording and document on it, in the order they were made. For when you know the date but not the folder.

This is one real Monday, exactly as Tracktiv drew it while it happened. Nobody started a timer, and nobody filled anything in afterwards.

How we work

Three rules we don't bend

Native, not wrapped

Swift and the frameworks Apple ships. An app should open fast, feel like the system it runs on, and cost the machine as little as it can.

Your data stays yours

Our apps keep what they know on your Mac and nowhere else. No account, no sync, no analytics — and in Tracktiv's case, no network permission at all.

Small and finished

We would rather ship a smaller thing that is done than a bigger one that is nearly done. Everything here is used every day by the person who wrote it.

The company

DevNest s.r.o.

DevNest is a software company registered in Slovakia. It began as a set of side projects, the first of which reached the Mac App Store in 2017, and grew into the place the apps on this site come from.

Alongside our own products we take on development work for other people: macOS and iOS apps, system-level tooling, and the parts of a project that turn out to be harder than they looked.

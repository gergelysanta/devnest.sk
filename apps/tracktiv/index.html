Tracktiv · Coming soon

You didn't lose the day.
Here is where it went.

Tracktiv is a time tracker for people who keep multiple projects going at once. It records your working day on its own, and in the evening it shows you what you worked on, for how long, and where the breaks were. It is not out yet: we are finishing it now.

For macOS No timers to start Nothing leaves your Mac

What it does

The day, written down for you

Nothing to remember

There is no timer to start and nothing to fill in later. Tracktiv sees which app and which window you work in, and writes the day down as it happens.

Every project apart

Each activity gets its own name and colour. You see how long each project took today, and how the whole month adds up.

Only on your Mac

No account, no sync, no analytics. Tracktiv has no network permission, so your day cannot leave the Mac.

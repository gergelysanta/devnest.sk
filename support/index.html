Reporting a bug

Four things make a bug report easy to act on, and any of them on its own is better than none:

  • Which app, and which version — every app shows it in Settings, under About, and Tracktiv also prints it with tracktivcli --version.
  • Your macOS version.
  • What you did, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see. ⇧⌘4 draws a rectangle; ⇧⌘4 then Space photographs one window.

If the app crashed, macOS has already written a report. Open Console, look under Crash Reports, find the newest entry with the app's name on it, and attach it.

Asking for a feature

Tell us the problem rather than the solution. "I cannot tell which of my three projects moved today" is more useful than "please add a pie chart" — it leaves room for an answer that fits the rest of the app, and it is the kind of message that has changed these apps before.

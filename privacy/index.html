Privacy policy

DevNest s.r.o. · last updated 11 September 2026

The short version: our apps keep what they know on your Mac, we have no server for them to talk to, and this website does not measure or track its visitors.

The apps

Tracktiv and AssetScout store their data locally, in the container macOS gives them on your own machine. Neither has an account, a login or a sync service, and neither sends what it records to us or to anybody else.

We therefore hold no personal data from the apps at all. There is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf, because there is nothing here. Deleting the app and its data from your Mac removes everything.

Tracktiv

Tracktiv is not built with the network permission macOS requires, so the system would refuse to let it reach the internet even if it tried. It records which application is in front of you and what its window is called, and it stores that on your Mac in order to draw your day. It does not read the contents of any document, and it does not use the Accessibility permission that would let it see what you type.

Where a trigger you set up asks Tracktiv to read a title bar as pixels, that reading happens on your Mac and neither the image nor the text is kept.

AssetScout

AssetScout reads the folders you point it at, and remembers which folders those were — in a security-scoped bookmark — so it can open them again after a restart. It reads the metadata stored in your files: the date a photograph was taken, the camera, the location if the file carries one. That reading happens on your Mac and the result stays there.

Purchases

AssetScout is free. Where an app of ours is sold, it is sold through the Mac App Store, so Apple handles the payment and the receipt. We never see your card details, your address or your Apple Account. What we do see is Apple's own sales reporting: how many copies sold, in which country, on which day. It names nobody.

This website

devnest.sk is a set of static files. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The appearance you choose with the sun and moon button is kept in your browser's local storage and never leaves it.

Two third parties see your visit, because they deliver the page to you. Cloudflare serves the site, so like any web host it sees your IP address and your browser. Google Fonts serves the two typefaces the page uses, so Google's font server also sees your IP address. If you would rather not load the fonts from Google, a content blocker will stop them, and the site still works in your system's own typeface.

Email

When you write to support@devnest.sk we keep your message for as long as it is useful — long enough to answer you, and to recognise the same bug when somebody else reports it. We do not add you to any mailing list, and there is none to add you to.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or erased, and complain to a supervisory authority. In practice the answer to the first question is almost always "your email, if you have written to us". Write to contact@devnest.sk and we will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The controller is DevNest s.r.o.; the company details are on the company page.

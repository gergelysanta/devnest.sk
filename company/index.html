Company

DevNest s.r.o.

A software company in Slovakia that builds for the Mac. We write native apps with the frameworks Apple ships, so they behave the way a Mac app should.

What we do

Most of our time goes into our own apps. Tracktiv is a time tracker. It records your working day on its own, and all of it stays on your Mac. AssetScout finds a file by the date its content was made, not by the folder it ended up in.

We also take on development work for clients: macOS and iOS apps, system-level tools, and the hard parts of a larger project. If you need something like that, write to contact@devnest.sk.

How we work

  • Native, not wrapped. We use Swift and the platform frameworks. An app should start fast, feel like part of the system, and use as little of the machine as it can.
  • Small and finished. We would rather ship a smaller thing that is done than a bigger one that is nearly done.
  • No data collection by default. If a feature does not need your data to leave your machine, it does not leave your machine. See the privacy policy for what that means in practice.
  • Reviewed by Apple. Our apps ship through the Mac App Store and TestFlight. Apple reviews every build, and every permission it asks for, before it reaches you.

A short history

DevNest started as evening and weekend work. Its first app went on the Mac App Store in 2017. The company was founded in 2023 to carry that work on, and it is still the same size: one person who writes the code and answers the email.

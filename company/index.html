Company

DevNest s.r.o.

A software company in Slovakia that builds for the Mac. Native software, made with the frameworks Apple ships, that behaves the way a Mac app is supposed to behave.

What we do

Most of our attention goes to our own apps. Tracktiv is the current one: a time tracker that records your working day on its own and hands it back to you at the end of it, without ever sending a byte anywhere. AssetScout is the other: it finds the file you saved months ago and cannot place, by the date its content was made rather than by the folder it ended up in.

Alongside our own products we take on development work for other people — macOS and iOS apps, system-level tooling, and the parts of a project that turn out to be harder than they looked. If that sounds like something you need, write to us.

How we work

  • Native, not wrapped. Swift and the platform frameworks. An app should start fast, feel like the system it runs on, and cost the machine as little as possible.
  • Small and finished. We would rather ship a smaller thing that is done than a bigger one that is nearly done.
  • No data collection by default. If a feature does not need your data to leave your machine, it does not leave your machine. See the privacy policy for what that means in practice.
  • Reviewed by Apple. Our apps ship through the Mac App Store and TestFlight, so every build and every permission it asks for is on record with someone other than us.

A short history

DevNest started as a set of evenings and weekends, and the first of those went on the Mac App Store in 2017. The company was formed around what came after it, and it has stayed the size it was: one person writing the code and answering the email.
